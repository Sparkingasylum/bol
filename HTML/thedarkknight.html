The dark knight

  • the dark knight is a 2008 superhero film directed, produced, and co-written by christopher nolan
  • it is the second installment of nolan's the dark knight trilogy and a sequel to 2005's batman begins
  • the film stars christian bale as bruce wayne / batman, heath ledger as the joker, a criminal mastermind who seeks to undermine batman's influence and create chaos in gotham city
  • the film also features michael caine, gary oldman, aaron eckhart, maggie gyllenhaal, and morgan freeman in supporting roles
